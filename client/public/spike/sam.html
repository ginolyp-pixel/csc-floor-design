SAM Spike — tap-to-segment floor detection

Phase 3 research spike. Upload a garage photo, tap once on the floor, watch the model produce a mask. Foreground tap = green dot (include this region). Hold Shift to add a background tap = red dot (exclude this region). Repeat to refine until the mask matches the floor.

Heads up: auto-detect is best on a tablet or computer. On phones the model may not load (memory limits) or the tab can reset mid-upload. If that happens, just use the manual trace tool in the main app — it works the same on any device.

Spike notes & instructions

What to evaluate on each photo:

  • Does a single tap on the floor produce a mask that follows the real floor edge?
  • How long does the image encoder take (one-shot per photo, scales with photo size)?
  • How long does the decoder take per tap (should be sub-200ms)?
  • Try photos with: clean empty floor / cars parked / tools strewn around / strong shadows / wide-angle phone shot.
  • When the auto-mask is wrong, can you fix it with one or two shift-click background taps?

The model is downloaded from Hugging Face on first load and cached in the browser's IndexedDB. Subsequent loads are instant.
